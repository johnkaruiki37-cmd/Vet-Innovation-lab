About Vet Innovation lab

The Vet Innovation Lab is a dedicated technical and research maker-space within the Faculty of Veterinary Medicine(FVM).We move beyond traditional discussions to actively prototype, test and deploy practical solutions for livestock health,disease surveillance, and veterinary practice in East Africa.

Our Motto:Think. Build. Test. Improve. We encourage members to build robust tools optimized for low-resource and field conditions.
